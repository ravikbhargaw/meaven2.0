Frameless Glass Shower Cabins

Option 02

Semi-Frameless & Sliding Enclosures

Engineered for high functionality in tight spaces. Uses slim anodized aluminum profiles to guide the glass panels, offering sliding openings that do not require door swing space.

  • 8mm or 10mm Toughened Safety Glass
  • Ultra-slim moisture-resistant track profiles
  • Smooth-glide bearing rollers for effortless movement
  • Excellent structural rigidity

Option 03

Walk-In Shower Screens

A minimalist single-panel design that segments your bathroom into distinct wet and dry zones. Ideal for open wet-rooms, this design eliminates the door entirely, leaving a sleek walk-in passage.
