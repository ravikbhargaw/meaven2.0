Glass Partition Cost Guide 2024: Budgeting for Bangalore Offices

May 12, 2026 • 6 min read

Planning an office fit-out in Bangalore? Understanding the cost of glass partitions is crucial for accurate budgeting. This guide breaks down the variables that influence pricing in 2024.

Key Factors Influencing Cost

The cost of a glass partition system isn't just about the glass itself. Several factors play a role in the final quote:

  • Glass Thickness: 10mm and 12mm toughened glass are industry standards. 12mm glass offers better rigidity and sound insulation but comes at a higher price point.
  • Frame System: Slim-profile aluminum frames (like our 45mm series) are popular for their aesthetic appeal. Frameless systems use heavier hardware, which can increase costs.
  • Hardware Quality: Floor springs, patch fittings, and door handles vary significantly in price based on brand and finish.

Typical Price Ranges in Bangalore

In the current Bangalore market, you can expect the following price ranges for standard installations:

  • Standard 45mm Glass Partitions: Starting from ₹350 to ₹450 per sqft.
  • Slim-Line Single Glazed: ₹450 to ₹600 per sqft.
  • Acoustic Double Glazed: ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 per sqft.

Hidden Costs to Watch For
