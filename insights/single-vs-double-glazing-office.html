Single vs. Double Glazing: Which is Right for Your Office Cabins?

May 12, 2026 • 5 min read

The choice between single and double glazing often comes down to one question: How much does acoustic privacy matter to you? We compare both systems to help you decide.

Single Glazing: The Minimalist Choice

Single glazed partitions are the most common choice for modern offices. Using 10mm or 12mm toughened glass, they provide a sleek, frameless look that maximizes transparency and natural light transfer.

  • Pros: Most cost-effective, fastest installation, maximizes natural light.
  • Cons: Limited acoustic insulation (typically STC 30-34). Conversations in the cabin can often be heard outside.

Double Glazing: The Acoustic Powerhouse

Double glazed systems use two layers of glass with an air gap between them. This creates an acoustic buffer that significantly reduces sound transmission across busy workspace floors.

  • Pros: Superior soundproofing (STC 45+), premium aesthetic, better thermal insulation.
  • Cons: Higher cost (often 2-3x single glazing), thicker profile frames.

When to Choose Which?

We generally recommend Single Glazing for general office dividers, breakout zones, and open-plan areas. Double Glazing is essential for executive cabins, boardrooms, and any space where confidential discussions take place.
