How We Work

We don't manufacture.
We coordinate — and we're accountable for every outcome.

Meaven is not a factory. We don't employ carpenters, electricians, or flooring crews on payroll. What we do is source, screen, and manage the best trade vendors in Bangalore — and we stand accountable for their output. Every vendor in our network has been vetted for quality, reliability, and GST compliance. When something goes wrong on site, you call us — not fifteen different contractors.
